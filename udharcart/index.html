FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is UdharKart?

UdharKart is a digital grocery ordering and credit management platform connecting customers and shopkeepers.

Is UdharKart free?

Yes, UdharKart is completely free for customers. Shopkeepers get a free trial with premium plans coming soon.

How does the OTP login work?

Enter your phone number, receive a real OTP via SMS, and verify. No fake or hardcoded OTPs.

Can I use UdharKart without internet?

UdharKart requires an internet connection to sync orders and update ledgers in real-time.
